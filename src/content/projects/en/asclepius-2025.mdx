The third ASCLEPIUS project targets **stroke detection and classification from CT images**. The architecture is a hybrid ensemble: **Vision Transformers** for global context and **Self-Organised Operational Neural Networks (SelfONN)** to model non-linear relationships.

The model reached a **0.91 F1 score on external validation sets**, well ahead of the classical baselines. Gains came from custom augmentation — specialised CutMix, Mosaic Augmentation and transparency techniques — plus automated region-of-interest detection and pre-processing.

Built in Python with PyTorch. The project qualified for the **Teknofest 2025** Artificial Intelligence in Health finals.
