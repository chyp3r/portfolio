You are reading on your balcony on a hot July day when your friend's portal swallows you and drops you into an icy land full of green creatures.

**Enyaroyis** is a 2D action RPG about the long journey home: make friends, fight the creatures, and push through to one of **four different endings**. It was a one-off "special production" — a single copy was made — so the page shows gameplay footage rather than a download.
