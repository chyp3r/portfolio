Under the supervision of Assistant Professor Engin Demir, I am developing a **mechanistic theory of artificial sociality** — how structure and diffusion emerge in networks of LLM agents.

The method builds a **hybrid semantic-topological network**: interaction frequency gives the topology, multilingual embeddings give the semantics, and the two are combined to analyse how information spreads.

The harder half of the work is showing the semantic effect is real. Robustness is tested with **degree-preserving null models**, **QAP-based ablation**, sensitivity analysis, cross-model evaluation, and adversarial **Sybil simulations**.

A manuscript is in preparation.
