For BabaMisu I built **CNN models that classify plant diseases into four classes from leaf images**. Data augmentation, regularisation and hyperparameter tuning brought the model to an **F1 score of 0.82**.

The trained model was integrated into a **Flutter + Firebase mobile app** for real-time, on-field diagnosis by farmers. The team completed every phase — documentation and presentation included — and the project **qualified for the Teknofest 2025 finals**.
