Vitabyte was my **Deneyap graduation project**. It builds a personalised diet plan from the health information and goals a user enters.

Unlike apps that hand out fixed diet lists, Vitabyte runs your data through a **custom AI recommender** that produces a plan for you specifically. Around that sit calorie tracking, recipes, sport tracking, suggestions and a social profile section.

As a graduation project it was never published publicly; the linked status report covers the final build.
