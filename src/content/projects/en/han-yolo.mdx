I worked on **real-time object detection for aerial images** from drone cameras, on an optimised **YOLOv8** backbone tuned for high-resolution footage in varying conditions.

The key idea was a **SLAM** approach that fixes object positions from visual data alone — no GPS — which makes the system usable in GPS-denied settings such as disaster zones. The pipeline mixed **PyTorch** and **TensorFlow**, with **Roboflow** for data preparation, developed under **WSL**.
