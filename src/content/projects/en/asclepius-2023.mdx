The project set out to build software that could **analyse mammography images**. We started with a literature review and wrote the first-stage report from it.

In the second stage we tested and compared several AI architectures and kept improving the results, documenting everything in a second report. The project placed **9th in Türkiye** in the report rankings, cleared the second stage, and went on to compete in the Teknofest final in Istanbul.
