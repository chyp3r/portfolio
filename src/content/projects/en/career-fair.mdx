I was a mobile developer on the official app for the **Hacettepe Career Fair**, one of the largest university career events in Türkiye — **15,000+ visitors and 150 companies**.

For this edition the app was **rebuilt from scratch in Flutter + Firebase**, adding live event tracking, company filters, personalised schedules and real-time notifications. The fair saw a **25% increase in visitor engagement** over the previous year.
