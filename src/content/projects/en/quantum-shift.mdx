The year is 2127. A disease called Quantum Shift is spreading, and its defining symptom — constant, involuntary teleportation — makes it deadly. Humanity's one defence is a wristwatch. So what happens when a patient loses theirs?

**Quantum Shift** is a 2D platformer puzzle about getting that watch back, past attacking robots, teleportation crises and a lot of other obstacles. Built for the **HUJAM23** game jam; available on itch.io.
