Hocus Focus tackles rising screen time with gamification. You set a focus window — 15, 30, 60 or 120 minutes, the intervals the World Health Organization suggests — and the app rewards you for staying away from the screen while you read, eat or work.

You can share sessions with friends, which turns a solo habit into a small social game and gives people a reason to keep going.
