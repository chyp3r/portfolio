Students ran the full design-thinking loop — empathise, define, ideate, prototype, test — and worked with 2D drawing, 3D modelling, 3D printers, laser cutters, CNC and wood-forming machines, exhibiting their work at a project festival. Our graduation brief was a new product to improve bus travel.
