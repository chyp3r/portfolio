As team lead and AI developer, I built a generative-AI news platform for automated video generation and content synthesis. A multi-model pipeline — **GPT, DALL·E, Whisper, Qwen and Gemini** — handled multimedia creation, while a **PostgreSQL vector recommender** delivered personalised news.

The system ran on a **Dockerised Django backend**, with a **React** web dashboard and a **React Native** mobile app for a consistent cross-platform experience.

Our team took **1st place** at the Anadolu Agency Media Technologies Hackathon, recognised for the approach to AI-driven media production.
