As team lead and computer-vision developer, I built an AI system for **breast-cancer analysis** that combined medical imaging with radiology reports. A **YOLOv8 + BART** architecture handled BI-RADS and calcification classification, and an **LSTM** model did Named Entity Recognition on the reports.

The team won the **Best Presentation Award** at the national finals and placed **3rd in Türkiye** in the analysis-report phase of Teknofest 2024 Artificial Intelligence in Health, with **0.55 mAP** and a **0.95 F1** score. Training and augmentation used PyTorch and Albumentations.
