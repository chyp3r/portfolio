Built during the **OBSS 2025 Codecamp internship**, Filmora is an iOS app for movie discovery and AI conversation. On an **MVVM** architecture it pulls rich data from the **TMDB API** — hero showcases, popular charts, cast detail — with **Moya** for networking.

Beyond browsing, it has a personalised favourites system and a **Gemini-powered "Movie Chat"** where you can discuss films, explore popular topics and get recommendations in a dedicated interface.
