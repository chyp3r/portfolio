A **scenario-based, modular training simulation** for teams in crisis management and emergency response. The structure makes it easy to plug in new scenarios so users can practise decision-making, analysis and intervention in realistic environments.

The **first scenario** models a Chemical, Biological, Radiological and Nuclear (CBRN) incident, chosen to show how the system trains people for high-risk situations.

The platform began as a **senior capstone project** at Hacettepe. I joined **voluntarily as a second-year student**, working on the **design of simulation environments** and **AI-assisted decision-support modules**.
